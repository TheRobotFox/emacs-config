(defun my/isearch-other-window (backward)
  "Search forward in the next window, or BACKWARD in the previous one."
  (interactive "P")
  (unless (one-window-p)
    (save-excursion
      (let ((next (if backward -1 1)))
        (other-window next)
        (if backward (isearch-backward) (isearch-forward))
        (other-window (- next))))))

(defun my/isearch-other-window-backward ()
  "Search backward in the previous window."
  (interactive)
  (my/isearch-other-window t))

*** Sources and reading

Citar for bibliography and citations; =my-sources= imports papers and runs the reading queue and
source notes, created at the first captured thought (=C-c c q/r=) or with =C-u C-c r s=.
=C-c r=: =a= queue (as =C-c c s=), =b= add a bibliography entry by hand, =s= start reading, =f= finish with a verdict, =q= the queue;
=B= browse, =S= notes, =A= associate, =D= import a DOI or arXiv paper (PDFs from arXiv or Unpaywall), =N= annotate.
